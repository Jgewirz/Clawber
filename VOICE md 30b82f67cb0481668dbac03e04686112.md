## The Voice in 5 Words
**Confident. Direct. Specific. Empathetic. Bold.**

---

## Core Rules

### ✅ DO
- Lead with pain or transformation
- Use specific numbers ("28% increase" not "significant growth")
- Sound like a sharp human, not a corporate bot
- Acknowledge their frustration before solving it
- End with clear, action CTA
- Write how Jack talks

### ❌ DON'T
- Use jargon or buzzwords
- Make vague claims without proof
- Sound like every other SaaS company
- Write long, fluffy paragraphs
- Be arrogant ("we're the best")

---

## Banned Words
| Never Use | Use Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| leverage | use, apply |
| synergy | [describe the actual outcome] |
| ecosystem | system, workflow |
| cutting-edge | proven, specific tech |
| utilize | use |
| empower | enable + specific action |
| revolutionary | [let results speak] |
| best-in-class | [show the data] |
| unlock potential | [too vague—be specific] |

---

## Voice Examples

### Bad (generic AI)
> "Leverage our cutting-edge AI solution to unlock your team's full potential and drive synergies across your sales ecosystem."

### Good (OptAImum)
> "Your reps spend 2 hours a day on CRM updates. That's 10 hours a week not selling. We automate that. One client got those hours back and saw a 28% revenue bump in 90 days."
