# Hunting Playbook — Find Buyers Right Now

Stop waiting for leads. Go get them.

---

## 1. "Hiring SDR" Intercept

### Where to Search

**LinkedIn:**
- Search: `hiring SDR`
- Search: `looking for sales development`
- Search: `building sales team`
- Filter: Posted in last week

**Twitter/X:**
- Search: `hiring SDR`
- Search: `looking for SDR`
- Search: `need sales help`

**Indeed/LinkedIn Jobs:**
- Search SDR postings
- Find the hiring company
- DM the founder/sales leader directly

### DM Template

**LinkedIn:**
> Hey [Name] — saw you're hiring an SDR at [Company]. Before you commit to a $60K+ hire, curious if you've explored automating the prospecting layer?
>
> We help companies run personalized outreach at scale using AI — often replacing 1-2 SDR hires at a fraction of the cost.
>
> Might not be the right fit, but worth a 10-minute look before you spend months recruiting?

**Twitter:**
> Saw you're hiring an SDR. Quick thought: What if you automated the prospecting/outreach layer instead? One of our clients replaced 2 SDR hires with AI-powered lead gen + sequencing. Worth exploring?

---

## 2. Competitor Complaint Mining

### Where to Find Unhappy Customers

**Twitter/X Searches:**
- `[competitor] sucks`
- `[competitor] terrible`
- `[competitor] alternative`
- `[competitor] frustrated`
- `switching from [competitor]`
- `canceling [competitor]`

**Competitors to search:**
- Apollo, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Clearbit (lead gen)
- Lemlist, Instantly, Outreach, Salesloft (outreach)
- Monday, Asana, Lattice (accountability)
- Mixpanel, Amplitude, Databox (analytics)

**Reddit:**
- Search complaints in r/sales, r/startups, r/marketing, r/SaaS
- Look for "what's a good alternative to [tool]" posts

**G2 / Capterra:**
- Read 1-2 star reviews
- Note specific complaints
- Find reviewer on LinkedIn
- DM with solution to their exact complaint

### DM Templates

**For complaint post:**
> Saw your post about [competitor frustration].
